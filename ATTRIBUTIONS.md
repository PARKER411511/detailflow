# DetailFlow image attributions

The hero and matching before/after concept images in `public/images/` were generated with the built-in Codex image generation tool for this fictional studio. The original prompt described a photorealistic wide low front three-quarter view of an unbranded royal-blue coupe in a deep navy premium studio, with cool strip lights and negative space on the left; the before image was edited from the same composition to add dusty road grime. They are portfolio concepts, not customer work, and are labeled as such in the UI.

The remaining gallery and interior images are illustrative stock photography from Unsplash. They are used as moodboard imagery and do not depict DetailFlow customers, staff, or verified results.
